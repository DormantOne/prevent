6

Picture for the patient's MyUPMC message

Copy this picture and paste it into a MyUPMC message. It fills in from this call: the medications reviewed, reminders, any eye exam or mammogram report still needed, and a QR code for reviewing preventive care.

If "Copy picture" is blocked, right-click the picture and choose Copy image, or use Download PNG.

Previsit note

Everything from this call, ready to paste into the previsit note in Epic. It updates as you fill in the steps above.
