Prostate Surveillance Navigator

Turns your pathology, PSA dynamics, imaging, biomarkers, age and background into a monitoring cadence, re-biopsy triggers, and a formal MRI-era probability estimate — then packages it as a copy-paste query for Open Evidence. Tap any ? for plain-language detail.

Not medical advice, not a validated calculator. The probability estimate is an illustrative, literature-anchored model (PSA-density + PI-RADS), not the proprietary PCPT/ERSPC/PBCG calculators, and outputs wide uncertainty bands by design. Use it to structure a conversation with a urologist and to vet against primary evidence.
